# Scientific Viewpoint Analysis
## Definining the scientific viewpoint
- From a scientific viewpoint the evolution of mobile phones to smartphones can be evaluated through the development of novel technologies, modification/adoption of pre-existing technologies, removal/replacement of redundant technologies and differences in the function of phones. Hence the distinction between mobile and smartphones can be illustrated through these differences.

### Differences in the functions of Phones
- Historically mobile phones could be distinguished by their rudimentary functions such as the ability to call, text and or play very simple games as such these phones may be known as feature phones. While there is no strict definition of a smartphone compared to feature phones, most distinctions are drawn when the hardware or Operating System (OS) within the phone can handle additional functionality besides the rudimentary functions of feature phones. Such definitions include the vague ability to run programs or applications besides said rudimentary features or the inclusion of networking hardware and sensors.

- These range to tighter definitions such as the ability to download and install third party applications, although such definitions would even exclude the first iPhone as a smartphone. iPhones only received this functionality in the second generation of iPhones. Despite the lack of clear a distinction, smartphones have distinguished themselves through the additional functionality they provide, hence their role has also shifted from simple communication devices becoming personal data storage devices, multimedia devices and in essence portable computers trading power for a smaller form factor. Notably this intent to create a phone with the capabilities of a home computer was held within the design philosophy of one of the first smartphones, the aformentioned IBM Simon.


### Development of novel technologies.\
1. Predictive text was a new OS feature introduced by the IBM Simon, unlike modern predictive text features the IBM Simon's PredictaKey Keyboard was a series of 6 letters which were displayed depending on what the phone predicted was the most likely to be used next. If the user was unsatisfied with the presented letters the 'other' button my be pressed to display the next set of likely 6 letters.
  

### Modification/adoption of previous technologies
1. Touch-screen is a preexisting technology, a touchscreen is defined as the ability for a surface area to simultaneously display an image or act as a 'screen' and receive an input. There are 2 forms of touch-screen, resistive touch-screen and capacitive touch-screen. Resistive touch-screen used pressure from a physical object to generate an input signal this was regularly done with a stylus as seen with the original IBM Simon touchscreen and stylus. Capacitive touch-screen identified changes in the electro-static field by physical interaction from an electrically conductive object.

- Most smartphones used resistive touch-screens prior to the first generation iPhone, the first generation iPhone popularized the use of capacitive touchscreens and developed touchscreen technology by overcoming sensitivity, gesture recognition and smooth glass interface limitations. The adoption of touch-screen required a new development in OS design and the expanse of UI/UX areas of research in science for interfaces expecting human touch within the small display dimensions of mobile phones.
  
2. Operating systems (OS) to run computer devices are a preexisting technology even on feature phones. While feature phones often ran a heavily limited OS, smartphones adopted an OS closer to a desktop style OS allowing for third party applications to be downloaded and ran on the OS and allowing for greater functionality especially when connected to networks such as the internet. 

### Removal/replacement of redundant technologies
1. Buttons were the primary input method for feature phones which were largely dropped in favor for the previously mentioned resistive and capacitive touchscreens. While most buttons were removed some physical buttons were still present such a navigation buttons on the bottom and media controls on the side for controlling volume or allowing quick switching on or off of the device.
