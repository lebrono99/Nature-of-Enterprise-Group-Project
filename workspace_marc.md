# Initial information
* Mobile phones may be considered/called **Feature Phones** when compared to smart phones.
* Feature phone technology ({**Wikpedia**} *Depending on extent of functionality, feature phones may have many of the capabilities of a smartphone, within certain cases. The hardware of feature phones often includes a backlit liquid-crystal display LCD screen, a hardware notification LED, a micro USB port, a physical keyboard, a microphone, a microSD card slot, a rear-facing camera to record video and capture pictures, and GPS services. Some feature phones include a rudimentary app store that includes basic mobile apps such as a calendar, calculator, mobile web, and mobile games. Following the rise of smartphones, the feature phone has sometimes been referred to as a dumbphone. The first GSM phones and many feature phones had NOR flash memory, from which processor instructions could be executed directly in an execute in place architecture and allowed for short boot times. With smartphones, NAND flash memory was adopted as it has larger storage capacities and lower costs, but causes longer boot times because instructions cannot be executed from it directly, and must be copied to RAM first before execution.*)

# What is a scientific viewpoint
* From a scientific viewpoint the evolution of mobile phones to smartphones can be evaluated through the development of novel technologies, modification/adoption of pre-existing technologies, removal/replacement of redundant technologies and differences in the function of phones. Hence the distinction between mobile and smartphones can be illustrated through these differences.
  
* Development of novel technologies.
  

* Modification/adoption of previous technolgies
 1: Touch-screen is a prexisting technology, a touchscreen is defined as the ability for a surface area to simultaniously display an image or act as a 'screen' and receive an input.There are 2 forms of touch screen, resistive touchscreen and capacitive touchscreen. resistive touchscreen used pressure from a physical object to generate an input signal. Capacitive touch screen identified changes in a electro-static field by physical interaction from an electrically conductive object (Cite Capacitive_touchscreen). Most mobile-phones used bottons or resistive touchscreen prior to the first generation iphone, the apple iphone popuarized the use of capacitvie touchscreens(citation needed) and developed touchscreen technology by overcoming sensitivity, gesture recogonition and smooth glass interface limitations.(cite Capacitive_touchscreen) The adoption of touchscreen required a new development in OS design and the expanse of UI/UX areas of research in science for interfaces expecting human touch within the small display dimensions of mobilephones.
 2: Operating system (OS) to run computer devices are a preexisting technology even on feature phones. While feature phones often ran a heavily limited OS, smartphones adopted an OS closer to a desktop style OS allowing for third party applications to be downloaded and ran on the OS and allowing for greater functionality especially when connected to networks such as the internet. 

* Removal/replacement of redundant technologies
 1: Buttons were the primary input method for feature phones which were largely dropped in favor for the previously mentioned resistive and capacitive touchscreens. 

* Differences in the functions of Phones
 1:There is no strict definition of a smartphone when compared to a feature phone, although most distinctions are drawn when the OS within the phone can handle additional functionality compared to rudimentary call, text and simple games of feature phones. Such definitions include the ability to run programs or applications besides said rudimentary features. This ranges to tighter definitions such as the ability to download and install third party applications, such definitions would even exclude the first iPhone as a smartphone, iPhones only received this functionality in the second generation of iPhones. Despite the lack of clear a distinction, smartphones have distinguished themselves through the additional functionality they provide, hence their role has also shifted becoming personal data storage devices, multimedia devices and in essence portable computers, trading power for a smaller form factor.
