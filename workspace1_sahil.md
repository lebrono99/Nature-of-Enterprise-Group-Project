- Evolution of the smartphone

- Historical development
  
  Nowadays, most of us here have smartphones which we use every day, but it wasn't always the case so today we will talk about the history of the smartphone and its development.
- So, to start, the first smartphone ever made was in 1992 by IBM engineer Frank Canova. It could do things like checking stocks, news, sports, weather, and more!
- Since then, in 1994, they made the 1st public available phone called the Simon and improved their previous model so that it could make telephone calls, send and receive emails, and it had apps like a calendar, appointment    scheduler, calculator, world clock, and notepad.
  It was also a touchscreen device, had a stylus and used lithium powered batteries like modern day smartphones. 
- However, the Simon ended up having poor sales because of its big heavy size and poor battery life, but it still revolutionized the tech world and inspired others to integrate on it.

- The next big jump after the Simon was the Ericsson R380 in 1999 (before Sony's partnership) and the BlackBerry 5810 in 2002. These were one of the first phones that allowed people to access the big internet, opening up a
  whole lot more things to do but both of them used old style buttons on part of the display unfortunately.

- The biggest revolutionary smartphone upgrade that most of us can agree on is the creation of the iPhone in 2007 which was announced by the original creator Steve Jobs. He even said to himself that "this is a day that I have been looking forward to for 2 and a half years," and that "today, Apple is going to reinvent the phone."
- In 2000, Steve Jobs envisioned the iPhone having a full touchscreen display without the need for a stylus or extra buttons which is how most phones are nowadays. The stylus was commonly used back in the day with touchscreen phones but removing it could make the experience less annoying and save money.
- 6 weeks after the iPhones release, the screen was replaced from plastic to glass after Steve Jobs was upset that his keys scratched the display while it was in his pocket. Every phone nowadays followed by using glass and avoiding other materials that would be worse for the display.
- The iPhone went on to be a huge success with over 6.1 million units being sold before being discontinued in July of 2008.

- This led to Apple helping to innovate other companies like Samsung to make their first phone and they would go on to be competitive rivals in the smartphone market.

- The first Samsung galaxy phone was announced and released in 2010. At the time, the Samsung galaxy S1 had the fastest graphical processing of any smartphone and was the thinnest smartphone at 9.9 mm.
  After being discontinued in 2012, the Samsung galaxy S1 ended up selling over 25 million units which is another huge jump. Part of its success at the time was because their operating system from Google, android was blowing up with more than 500 million android devices being used everyday. It's still the most used smartphone operating system to this day.

- The global smartphone audience surpassed 1 billion in 2012 and 1.75 billion in 2014. Nowadays it is said to be over 5 billion.
