Evolution of the smartphone

Historical development
- Most of us here have smartphones which we use everyday but i'm going to take you a trip down memory lane and show you the history of the smartphone and its development.
So to start, the first smartphone ever made was in 1992 by IBM engineer Frank Canova. It could do things like checking stocks, news, sports, weather and more!
- Since then in 1994, they made the 1st public available phone called the Simon and improved their previous model so that it could make telephone calls, send and receive emails, and it had apps like a calendar, appointment scheduler, calculator, world clock, and notepad. It was also touchscreen, had a stylus and used lithium powered batteries like modern day smartphones. 
- However, the Simon ended up having poor sales because of it's big heavy size and poor battery life.
