- Historical development
Nowadays, most of us here have smartphones which we use everyday but it wasn't always the case so today we will talk about the history of the smartphone and its development.
- So to start, the first smartphone ever made was in 1992 by IBM engineer Frank Canova. It could do things like checking stocks, news, sports, weather and more!
- Since then in 1994, they made the 1st public available phone called the Simon and improved their previous model so that it could make telephone calls, send and receive emails, and it had apps like a calendar, appointment    scheduler, calculator, world clock, and notepad.
  It was also touchscreen, had a stylus and used lithium powered batteries like modern day smartphones. 
- However, the Simon ended up having poor sales because of it's big heavy size and poor battery life but it still revolutionized the tech world and inspired others to integrate on it.

- The next big jump after the Simon was the Ericsson R380 in 1999 (before Sony's partnership) and the BlackBerry 5810 in 2002. These were one of the first phones that allowed people to access the big internet, opening up a
  whole lot more things to do but both of them used old style buttons on part of the display unfortunately.

- The biggest revolutionary smartphone upgrade that most of us can agree on is the creation of the IPhone in 2007 which was announced by the original creator Steve Jobs.
Steve Jobs envisioned the IPhone having a full touchscreen display without the need for a stylus or extra buttons, most phones nowadays are the same.
