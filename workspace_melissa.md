Smartphones have become an important part of our everyday life, changing the way we communicate, access information and interact with the world. Before smartphones, mobile phones were mainly used for calls and text messages, (https://www.bbc.co.uk/bitesize/articles/z62gjfr) but advances in science and technology have transformed them into devices that can preform many different functions, such as playing games, watching movies, online shopping and more. This evolution is significant because it has influenced both society and the way people live. We can see this from our own experience with everyday life. Smartphones have allowed us to get comfortable, we use our phones for information and entertainment on a daily basis, spending a lot of time on apps like TikTok, Snapchat, Instagram and Youtube. A lot of people in this room probably check their phone first thing when they wake up to turn off an alarm, check messages, or something within that region. This is a big evolution compared to when mobile phones were around. However, with comfort comes concern. And this project will explore this evolution from a scientific and social perspective, looking at the technological developments behind them and the positive and negative effects they have on everyday life.
