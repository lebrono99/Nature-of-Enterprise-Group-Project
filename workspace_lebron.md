D (Ethical): There are many ethical problems when it comes to mobile phones. One ethical problem is the manufacturing of the mobile phones. The material made for electronic parts from mobile phones comes from poor, corrupt countries. The materials are rare and can be found in Africa. The miners supplying these rare earth materials are working in extremely harsh conditions. They receive little to no pay, and the workers can be young as seven. These are happening in African countries like the Democratic Republic of Congo. Where miners are forced to work in the tunnels for as long as 24 hours straight. They work with little to no safety equipment, only working in normal clothes. They do not have safety helmets for protections, only wearing headlights to see what is in front of them. Most workers are not wearing any face mask, letting them to breathe in harmful dust that will affect their health in the long term. Working in the mines without basic safety equipment will majorly affect their mental and physical health. 
For example, people in Democratic Republic of Congo, a 14 year old boy named Paul was forced to mine cobalt in the mines for 24 hours straight in the tunnels. He started mining at the age of 12, reporting that the constant work in the underground mines made him always ill and sick. 
The material like cobalt is then exported to different countries like China and Korea. Where it is then used to make smartphones and batteries for electric cars.
